## 📌 Executive Summary & Key Metrics

* **Total Revenue:** `138.48M SAR` *(+105.0% MoM Growth vs. previous month's 67.55M SAR)*
* **Total VAT Collected:** `18.06M SAR` *(Accounting for 28% of overall tax-attributed sales)*
* **Invoicing Conversion Rate:** `72%` *(805 Invoiced Orders out of 1,112 Total Orders)*
* **Credit Risk Exposure:** `$77.9M` SAR across 45 high-risk customer accounts exceeding credit thresholds.

---
## 📖 Introduction & Problem Statement

In today's fast-paced commercial environment, managing revenue growth without controlling financial exposure poses a critical risk to business sustainability. Traditional reporting often evaluates sales metrics in isolation, overlooking pending order fulfillments, unpaid tax distributions, and customer credit defaults.

This project delivers an end-to-end **Sales Performance & Credit Risk Intelligence Solution**. Built using **Power BI** and advanced **DAX modeling**, it integrates commercial sales operations with financial risk management—providing C-suite executives with dynamic, real-time visibility into revenue trends, invoicing conversion efficiency, customer credit limits, and individual sales force performance.

---

## 🔑 Key Features & Analytical Pillars

* **📊 Executive Sales Overview:** Interactive tracking of month-over-month revenue surges, regional location sales performance (led by top area with 42.7M SAR), and fulfillment status (Invoiced, Open Order, Delivered).
* **👥 Customer Performance & Risk Exposure:** Granular customer segmentation pinpointing top revenue contributors alongside financial risk metrics to prevent bad debt accumulation.
* **👔 Salesperson Efficiency & Ranking:** Automated evaluation and category classification of sales representatives based on order volume, total revenue share, and individual conversion rates.

## 💡 Strategic Recommendations

1. **🔒 Tighten Credit Limits for High-Risk Accounts:** Implement automated credit-block triggers for the 45 accounts currently exceeding credit limits to prevent cash flow bottlenecks and mitigate the $77.9M credit exposure.
2. **⚡ Accelerate Order Invoicing Workflows:** Streamline processing between "Open Orders" (38.77%) and "Invoiced Orders" (60.81%) to raise the current 72% invoicing rate closer to the 85%+ target.
3. **🎯 Replicate Top Sales Rep Strategies:** Leverage operational insights from top-performing representatives (e.g., Ahmed Ameer contributing 19% total sales share) to establish best practices across lower-performing regional teams.
4. **📍 Expand High-Performing Geographic Zones:** Direct marketing and distribution resources toward top-tier regions generating over 31% of total sales volume to maximize ROI.
