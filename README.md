An executive-level data analytics solution designed to track **Sales Performance**, **Credit Risk Exposure**, and **Salesperson Efficiency**. This dashboard bridges financial analytics and sales operations to drive data-backed decision-making.

---

## 📌 Executive Summary

* **Total Sales:** `138.48M SAR` *(+105.0% MoM Growth)*
* **Total VAT Amount:** `18.06M SAR`
* **Invoicing Rate:** `72%` *(Total Orders: 1,112)*
* **Credit Risk Exposure:** `$77.9M` across 45 high-risk accounts.

---

## 🔑 Key Features & Insights

* **📊 Sales & Revenue Overview:** Real-time tracking of MoM sales growth, order statuses, and location-based revenue distribution.
* **👥 Customer Performance & Credit Risk:** Granular customer segmentation identifying top buyers and evaluating credit risk limits to mitigate defaults.
* **👔 Salesperson Analytics:** Ranking top-performing sales representatives, tracking individual order volume, and monitoring target achievements.
